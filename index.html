How It Works

👤 Worker Journey

  1. Send a WhatsApp message to Oswok AI
  2. Sabi AI greets you in a warm, local voice
  3. Register: name, phone, gender, district, skills
  4. Get a personalized learning pathway
  5. Complete lessons, take quizzes, unlock micro-jobs
  6. Submit proof-of-work (GPS + photo)
  7. Earn to wallet, withdraw via Mobile Money

🏢 Organization Journey

  1. Sign up as an organization or business
  2. Post jobs — AI decomposes into micro-tasks
  3. Pay workers via Mobile Money through the platform
  4. Browse vetted service providers with Work Passports
  5. Chat privately with candidates
  6. Leave reviews and build trust networks
  7. AI handles matching, verification & payment
