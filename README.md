# Oswok AI — The AI-Powered Economic Mobility Engine

> No CV. No office. Just chat, learn, work, and get paid.

Oswok AI is an AI-powered workforce operating system delivered entirely through WhatsApp. It transforms human potential into verified income opportunities — skill discovery, micro-learning, verified tasks, and a Digital Work Passport — built for underserved youth across Africa.

## 🧠 The Problem

Sierra Leone youth face massive unemployment and underemployment. Traditional job platforms require CVs, office visits, and internet access — barriers that exclude most youth. Skills go unverified, income opportunities are informal and unreliable, and there's no accessible pathway from learning to earning.

## ✨ The Solution

Three core pillars, all on WhatsApp:

1. **AI Career Pathway** — Personalized skill discovery powered by AI
2. **Learn → Verify → Earn** — Micro-learning with quizzes and verified tasks
3. **Digital Work Passport** — A portable, verified work history

## 📱 How It Works

### Worker Journey
1. Send a WhatsApp message to Oswok AI
2. Sabi AI greets you in a warm, local voice
3. Register: name, phone, gender, district, skills
4. Get a personalized learning pathway
5. Complete lessons, take quizzes, unlock micro-jobs
6. Submit proof-of-work (GPS + photo)
7. Earn to wallet, withdraw via Mobile Money (NLe 50–1,500)

### Organization Journey
1. Sign up as an organization or business
2. Post jobs — AI decomposes into micro-tasks
3. Pay workers via Mobile Money through the platform
4. Browse vetted service providers with Work Passports
5. Chat privately with candidates
6. Leave reviews and build trust networks
